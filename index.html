What this book is.

A proposal for Mark Cuban Companies: the public portfolio at markcubancompanies.com, rebuilt as a LifeBook to tap, search and hear read aloud.

The logos and photographs are from markcubancompanies.com and load from that site. The words come from the public list and from news reports, read on 14 November 2026. Nothing here is published, and his team has not reviewed it yet.

Listed is not the same as owned. The list gives no stakes and no dates, so the best-known holdings were checked against outside sources on pages 6 and 7. The rest appear as the site lists them.

The format

What a LifeBook can do

1Every name is a tapA card opens with the logo, what the company does and where to find it.
2The whole book is searchablePage 5 finds any of the 175 by name or by tag.
3It reads itself aloudListen speaks the open pages in the reader's own browser.
4Every page has its own linkSend someone straight to a section or a single page.
5Sponsors get a page that actsOne tap from the page to the offer. See the sample on page 16.

It opens in a browser on a phone, a tablet or a laptop. Nothing to install.

About the list

Small print worth reading

Descriptions are short paraphrases of the one-line tagline each company has on the official site. They are the companies' own words about themselves, shortened.

Website links are the ones the official site gives. They were not opened one by one. Seven companies have no link there, so none is shown.

Twelve companies have no industry on the site. They are in the last section.

The official list leaves out companies he has sold and, by one report, companies that no longer exist.
